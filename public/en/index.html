Profile

Reporting on the Church, understanding how journalism is changing

Riccardo Benotti is an Italian professional journalist and author. He has worked since 2006 at SIR, the Servizio Informazione Religiosa, where he is now a senior editor. He covers the Pope, the Holy See and the life of the Church in Italy and around the world, and is accredited to the Holy See Press Office.

He holds a degree in Social Communication, specialising in journalism, from the Pontifical Salesian University in Rome, where he also earned his doctorate in 2025. His research looks at communication, culture and society, with a focus on how artificial intelligence is reshaping the work of newsrooms.

He has sat on the Film Commission of the Italian Bishops’ Conference since 2020, now for the 2024–2029 term, and contributes to the Conference’s interdisciplinary study group on disability. He writes for the Messaggero di sant’Antonio and teaches courses and workshops on digital communication, editorial planning and artificial intelligence.

Research

His doctoral thesis, defended in 2025 at the Pontifical Salesian University, examines the arrival of artificial intelligence in Italian Catholic newsrooms through Marshall McLuhan’s tetrad. The work continues in peer-reviewed articles on algorithmic discernment and journalistic authority.

Thesis
Intelligenza artificiale e giornalismo. Un’indagine sui pro e contro di una possibile implementazione di sistemi IA nelle redazioni cattoliche della Cei e della Santa Sede
Doctorate
Faculty of Social Communication Sciences, Pontifical Salesian University, Rome, defended on 17 December 2025. Supervisor: Prof. Fabio Pasqualetti. UPS thesis archive
Abstract
A qualitative study based on semi-structured interviews with journalists, editors and experts at Avvenire, Tv2000, SIR and Vatican News. McLuhan’s tetrad is used to read what AI enhances, makes obsolete, retrieves and reverses across the three stages of newsroom work: gathering, producing and distributing the news. The conclusions propose a model of integration that keeps human responsibility, and mediation as an act of discernment, at the centre.

For event organisers

A short biography for introductions and programmes, and a high-resolution photograph.

Riccardo Benotti is an Italian journalist and senior editor at SIR – Servizio Informazione Religiosa, the news agency of the Italian Bishops’ Conference, where he has worked since 2006. He is accredited to the Holy See Press Office. He holds a PhD in Social Communication from the Pontifical Salesian University in Rome and studies how artificial intelligence is transforming journalism. His books include Covid-19: preti in prima linea (San Paolo, 2021) and Viaggio nella vita religiosa (Libreria Editrice Vaticana, 2016). He is a member of the Film Commission of the Italian Bishops’ Conference.
